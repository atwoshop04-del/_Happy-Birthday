FOR YOU, WITH LOVE ♡

Happy birthday, Niel ❤️ ,

I hope you know how much you deserve good things in this life. I know I don’t always say it, but I’m genuinely grateful that I got to know you and have you in my life.

I hope this new chapter brings you closer to the things you’ve been working so hard for. I hope your studies, your dreams, your plans, and all the little things you’re trying to build slowly fall into place. And whenever things get overwhelming, I hope you remember that you don’t have to have everything figured out right away.

Please take care of yourself too, okay? Eat properly, get enough rest, and don’t push yourself too hard. I know you’re busy and you have so many things going on, but you deserve to rest too.

I’m really proud of you for making it this far, even through the days that probably weren’t easy. I hope you’ll have more reasons to smile this year, more peaceful days, more unforgettable memories, and people around you who genuinely care about you.

And selfishly… I hope I can still be one of those people who gets to be around for some of those moments.

Happy birthday again, Niel. Please stay the same annoying, hardworking, and wonderful person that I somehow ended up caring about this much. ❤️

I hope this year is kind to you. You deserve that.
